About Vision Tech Solutions

Your trusted partner in digital innovation, delivering high-impact IT solutions across Africa and beyond.

Our Background & History

Vision Tech Solutions was founded in 2020 in Kampala, Uganda, by a group of passionate IT engineers and entrepreneurs who envisioned a future where local businesses could access world-class technology services without complexity. What started as a small freelancing team has evolved into a full-scale IT company known for reliability, affordability, and innovation.

Driven by the need for digital transformation in education, healthcare, and commerce, we quickly expanded our expertise — from building simple websites to developing custom management systems, cybersecurity frameworks, and mobile applications. Over the years, we've partnered with schools, hospitals, startups, and corporations to simplify their operations through smart software solutions. Our journey is fueled by a commitment to bridge the technology gap and empower Ugandan and East African enterprises with sustainable digital tools.

Today, Vision Tech Solutions stands as a modern IT hub, blending creativity with technical excellence to solve real-world problems. Our team remains dedicated to continuous learning and delivering measurable results for every client.

Our Mission
